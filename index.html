🚅 JR Pass Verdict

Skip JR Pass — Save money!
A 7-day JR Pass costs ¥50,000/person (¥100,000 for 2). Your two Shinkansen legs total ¥56,180. The pass only saves money if you take 3+ long-distance rides in 7 days. For local trains, use IC cards (Suica/PASMO in Tokyo, ICOCA in Kansai).

🎌 Etiquette

  • 🚫 No tipping — it can be considered rude
  • 👟 Remove shoes when entering homes, temples, some restaurants
  • 🤫 Keep quiet on trains — silence phones, no calls
  • 🚶 Walk on the left (Tokyo) or right (Osaka)
  • 🗑 Carry your trash — few public bins
  • 🙏 Bow slightly when greeting or thanking
  • 💴 Use both hands when giving/receiving money or cards
  • 🍜 Slurping noodles is polite — shows enjoyment

♨ Onsen & Temple Rules

  • 🚿 Wash thoroughly before entering onsen bath
  • 🩱 No swimwear in traditional onsen (naked bathing)
  • 🖼 Tattoos may be restricted — check ahead or use private onsen
  • ⛩ Bow before entering shrine gate (torii)
  • 📸 Check photo rules at temples — some restrict interiors
  • 🧹 Walk on the sides of shrine paths (center is for gods)

💴 Money Tips

  • 🏧 7-Eleven ATMs accept foreign cards (low fees)
  • 💳 Cards accepted at major stores; cash still needed for small shops
  • 🛍 Tax-free: ¥5,000+ at participating stores, show passport
  • 📱 Suica in Apple Wallet works great for transit + konbini
  • 💰 Carry ¥10,000–20,000 cash for markets, shrines, small eateries

🌀 September Weather & Typhoons

  • 🌡 28–32°C, very humid — stay hydrated
  • 🌧 Afternoon thunderstorms common — carry compact umbrella
  • 🌀 Peak typhoon season — monitor weather apps
  • 🚄 Shinkansen may stop during typhoons — have backup plans
  • 👕 Light, breathable clothing; quick-dry fabrics ideal
